SANDLINE is a round-based tactical FPS in the browser: a desert compound with two bombsites, five patrolling defenders that see, hear and shoot back, a bomb to plant and defend, and money between rounds to buy rifles, SMGs, grenades and armour. Headshots kill, stopping to aim pays, and the defenders converge on a planted bomb to defuse it. Built with three.js 0.187.0, no build step, no network at runtime.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click to play. WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, 1/2 to switch weapons, G to throw a grenade, hold E to plant the bomb at the marked site, Ctrl to crouch, Space to jump, B for the buy menu.
